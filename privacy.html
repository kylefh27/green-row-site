Privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Green Row is a personal planning app for iPhone and Mac. This page explains what it stores, where, and who can see it. In short: your data is yours, it's used only to run the app for you, and it's never sold or used for ads.

What Green Row stores

  • Your account: your email address, and your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google. Green Row asks Google only for your basic profile and email.
  • What you put in the app: tasks, routines, when you completed them and how long they took, and your settings.
  • Calendar busy times: the app reads your calendars on your iPhone to find free time. The start, end and title of today's events are synced to your account so Green Row on your Mac shows the same free time. Other calendar details are never read or stored.
  • Location: if you set a home location, the app only stores whether you're home or away. It never keeps a history of where you've been.

Where it's stored

Your data is saved on your devices and, if you create an account, in a private database hosted by Supabase. Only you can read your data; access is restricted to your signed-in account. If you connect an iCloud Drive folder, a copy is also kept there, in your own iCloud.

Who can see it

  • Only you, by default.
  • Buddies you add: if you add a buddy, they see the name you choose, your streak, and today's squares (done, resting or still to do). They never see task or routine names.
  • Green Row does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and has no ads or third-party tracking.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Green Row receives your name, email address and Google account ID, and uses them only to create and identify your account. It doesn't access any other Google data. Green Row's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes your data from that device. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email kfratellohakim@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions: kfratellohakim@gmail.com.
